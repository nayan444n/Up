# Premium 100-Level Galaxy Map

Applied the new level-select direction:

- 100 levels remain available, split into 10 visual zones of 10 levels.
- The map is one continuous vertical scroll instead of a dense grid.
- Large vertical spacing keeps level nodes visually separated on mobile.
- Roughly 6–10 nodes are visible per viewport depending on device height; scrolling reveals the rest.
- S-shaped alternating left/right route gives the map a premium adventure-path feel.
- Each level has 3-star completion display, lock state, current state, and play affordance.
- Milestone levels (10, 20, …, 100) are visually marked as boss levels; 100 is the final boss.
- Character checkpoint art appears beside zones without adding top/bottom navigation bars.
- The level screen itself has no persistent header/footer HUD; only a floating close control remains.
- Home artwork is not modified by this change.
