# Privacy Policy — Galaxy Defender

**Publisher:** Finonix Software  
**Effective date:** August 11, 2026

Galaxy Defender does not require an account and the current release does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

## Data stored locally
Gameplay progress, settings, statistics, achievements, and related preferences are stored locally on the player's device. This data is used only to provide game functionality.

## Network and third parties
The current release does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, account services, camera, microphone, contacts, location, or cloud-save services. Any future data-collecting service will be disclosed in an updated policy before that release is distributed.

## Retention and deletion
Local game data remains on the device until the player resets game progress, clears app/browser storage, or uninstalls the app. Settings provides a reset-progress control.

## Security
The current release keeps gameplay data local and does not transmit it to the publisher.

## Contact
For privacy questions, use the developer contact information shown on the official Google Play listing for Galaxy Defender.

## Changes
Material changes to data practices will be reflected in an updated policy with a new effective date.
