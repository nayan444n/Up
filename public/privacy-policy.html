Privacy Policy — Galaxy Defender

Publisher: Finonix Software
Effective date: August 11, 2026

Summary

Galaxy Defender is an arcade game. The current release does not require an account and does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

Data stored on your device

The game stores gameplay progress, settings, statistics, achievements, and related preferences locally on your device using browser/app storage. This data is used only to provide game functionality.

Network and third parties

The current game does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, account services, camera, microphone, contacts, location, or cloud-save services. If a future release adds a service that collects or shares data, this policy will be updated before that release is distributed.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you reset the game's saved progress, clear the app/browser storage, or uninstall the app. The game provides a reset-progress control in Settings.

Children

The game does not knowingly request personal information from children.

Security

Gameplay data is stored locally and is not transmitted to the publisher by the current release.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the developer contact information shown on the official Google Play listing for Galaxy Defender.

Changes

Material changes to data practices will be reflected in an updated policy with a new effective date.
